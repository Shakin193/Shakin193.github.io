basketball_game.mp4: This is a basketball game built in XR space in Unity. First the player is in the real world, or an AR world because there's a virtual basketball in the real scene. When you grab the ball, which serves as a trigger object, there appears to be a corridor in a stadium, and I use locomotion technique here so that player can actually walk in the virtual scene by simply control the controller. And the corridor uses the stencil buffer technique. Then you can play the basketball game in the stadium like shooting or tapping the ball. For the basket net I used the cloth material and add the cloth constraints on it to make it act naturally. And since the stadium is a really big object file, I also used a lot of skills and debugging technologies to keep the game fluent.\

Instead of using a stick to control the white ball, players can directly control the write ball using the arrow buttons. vr_project_Thaihouse.mp4 is a haunting game I made with other two group mates. The whole vr scene is that you are following a creepy cat into a haunting Thai house, and you need to find a red paper to stick on the door to get back to normal. The red paper is stored in one box among different sizes and locations of boxes. A key stored in a pot is essential to open the boxes. Techniques of trigger volumes(for all the trigger boxes), animation(the walking cat, the opening box, the red paper and so on), global volume(for the haunting atmosphere) and so on are widely used in this game.\
