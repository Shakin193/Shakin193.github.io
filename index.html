01 — Profile

Profile

I'm Yiqun Zhang, a senior studying Computational Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK).

My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, 3D graphics, and interactive systems. I've spent the past two years building systems that bridge graphics programming and machine learning — from writing a complete WebGPU rendering pipeline from scratch to researching physical property estimation for retail products using multi-modal neural networks.

In Summer 2026, I conducted research at National Tsing Hua University (NTHU) in Taiwan, where I improved PSViT — a world model-inspired Vision Transformer for estimating physical mass from 3D point clouds and multi-view images.

I also spent Fall 2025 as an exchange student at Dartmouth College, where I built VR scenes and learned real-time graphics programming with WebGPU and Unity.
